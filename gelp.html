InshuVerse AI • Help Guide
Just press Spacebar. Speak. Get help. That’s it.

Quick Start

  • Sign in with Google.
  • Allow Microphone (and Screen Recording on Mac for screenshots/app audio).
  • Click Provide Profile to teach InshuVerse AI about your needs.
  • Press Spacebar to talk, or type in the chat box.

Interview Help

Paste your About You and optional Job Description. InshuVerse AI crafts confident answers, structures talking points, and helps with English.

  • Use Profile tab for your summary.
  • Use Job tab to tailor answers.
  • Ask: “Give me 3 bullet points for ‘Tell me about yourself’.”

Meetings

Describe your meeting context. Get live guidance, suggested phrasing, and quick summaries.

  • Fill Meeting Context and select Meeting Type.
  • Ask in real time: “How to disagree politely about scope?”

Sales Calls

Paste your pitch, company details, goals, and audience. InshuVerse AI coaches discovery questions and objection handling.

  • Tab between Pitch and Company.
  • Ask: “How to handle ‘too expensive’?”

ChatGPT Mode

Set a custom prompt and assistant role. InshuVerse AI replies in your chosen style.

  • Write your Custom Prompt and pick Assistant Role.
  • Example: “Act as a concise technical coach.”

Voice, Chat, and Screenshots

  • Voice: Press Spacebar or click the mic to start/stop listening.
  • Chat: Type in the box and press Enter.
  • Screenshot: Click the camera to capture your screen for instant analysis (enable Screen Recording on Mac).

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Spacebar — Start/Stop listening. In Settings you can switch to “Hold to record”.
  • Cmd/Ctrl + J — Switch to Chat mode and focus the input.
  • Cmd/Ctrl + R — Reset the conversation.
  • Cmd/Ctrl + H — Capture and analyze a screenshot.
  • Cmd/Ctrl + B — Hide/Show the InshuVerse AI window (works globally).

Tip: Spacebar is the fastest way to talk. Use the other shortcuts to keep flow during calls or interviews.

Permissions

  • Microphone is needed for voice input.
  • Screen Recording (Mac) is needed for screenshots and some app audio.
  • You can Skip and still use text chat; re-enable later in system settings.

Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording/Microphone → enable for InshuVerse AI, then restart the app.

Tips

  • Use short, clear questions. InshuVerse AI remembers your profile to tailor help.
  • For interviews, practice aloud. Ask InshuVerse AI to critique and improve your answer.
  • For meetings/sales, keep the app handy and ask for phrasing live.
  • Long text is auto-summarized for speed. Your originals aren’t lost.

Spacebar: Listening and Answer Flow

  • Press Spacebar to START listening. InshuVerse AI listens to your selected input (mic or loopback device) and the UI shows that it’s recording.
  • Speak clearly or let the selected input capture the conversation/audio.
  • Press Spacebar again to STOP. InshuVerse AI immediately processes what it heard and prepares the best answer.
  • You’ll see a concise, precise response with steps or phrasing you can use right away.

Audio Input: Choose and Test

Pick the device you want InshuVerse AI to listen to: your microphone for speaking, or a virtual/loopback device for better Results.

  • macOS: In most cases, selecting Default works best. For capturing system audio, use a loopback device like BlackHole or Loopback, then set that as your input in macOS and in InshuVerse AI.
  • Windows: For system audio, enable and select Stereo Mix (if available) in Sound settings. If Stereo Mix isn’t present, use a virtual cable (e.g., VB-Audio Cable) and select that in InshuVerse AI.
  • In InshuVerse AI Settings → Audio Input, pick the matching device and click Test to verify levels.
  • Speak at normal volume or play a sample. Watch the audio meter for steady movement without redlining.
  • If levels are too low, increase input gain; if too high/distorted, reduce it.

Tip: Try a short 3–5 second capture with Spacebar to confirm you get a good, timely answer before important meetings or interviews.

Aptitude Mode

A mode optimized for online coding/aptitude platforms and proctored tests (e.g., Salesforce PD1/PD2). It streamlines the UI and focuses on quick, crisp answers.

  • What changes: The mic becomes secondary; screenshot/camera actions are emphasized for fast problem capture.
  • When to use: During coding challenges, multiple-choice tests, or timed quizzes where you need rapid guidance.
  • How to enable: Open Settings → toggle Aptitude Mode. InshuVerse AI remembers your choice.
  • Best practice: Use short, specific prompts or screenshots of the question. Ask for a final answer plus a 2–4 line explanation.
  • Limits: Always follow your exam rules. Use InshuVerse AI for learning and preparation in allowed contexts.

Tip: Combine with Screenshot for the fastest extraction of the question and a succinct solution outline.

Use Cases and Example Prompts

  • Interviews: “Give me a 60–90 sec answer for ‘Tell me about yourself’ based on my profile.”
  • Behavioral: “Turn this story into STAR format with 3 crisp bullets.”
  • Technical: “Summarize the problem they asked and outline an approach in 4 bullets.”
  • Meetings: “Suggest a polite way to push back on unrealistic timeline.”
  • Sales calls: “I got a price objection. Give me two ways to respond and one discovery question.”
  • Docs/Slides: “Summarize this slide and give me three talking points with transitions.”
  • Learning/Language: “Explain what they just said in simpler English and give me a short reply.”
  • Coding/Debugging: “From what I described, list 3 likely root causes to check.”

Troubleshooting

  • Mic denied? Re-open app after allowing in system settings.
  • No screenshots on Mac? Enable Screen Recording for InshuVerse AI and restart.
  • Still stuck? Reboot the app or check your network.
